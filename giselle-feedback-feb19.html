🛠️ Three Keys to Level Up

1. 🦴 The "Straight Arm" Rest

I noticed your arms stay slightly bent even when you're just pausing to look for the next hold. Every second with bent arms burns your forearm fuel.

Homework: Next time you pause, consciously drop your weight into "long arms." Imagine you're hanging from a coat hanger. Let your skeleton do the work!

2. 🎯 Traverse Precision

On the traverse (where you had that dynamic reach), the fall usually starts with the feet. If your foot isn't 100% "glued" to the best part of the hold before you jump, the move won't have direction.

  • Look at the foot placement.
  • Place the toe tip precisely.
  • Weight it fully BEFORE reaching.

3. 🧘 Hips to the Wall

Keeping your "belly button to the wall" prevents your feet from swinging out (cutting) during big moves. Think about keeping your center of gravity stacked over your feet at all times.

📉 Fall Analysis: The Traverse Dyno
